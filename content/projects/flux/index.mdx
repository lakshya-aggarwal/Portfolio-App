## The problem

Most collaboration tools are optimistic about the network. They assume a message
sent is a message received, and the first time someone's train enters a tunnel
the document silently forks.

## Approach

Every mutation is an intent with a monotonic sequence number, applied locally
first and reconciled on acknowledgement. When a client reconnects it replays
from its last known sequence rather than refetching the world - which is what
keeps reconnection feeling instant instead of like a page load.

## What I'd do differently

I hand-rolled the reconciliation before reaching for a CRDT. Writing it taught
me exactly which guarantees I needed, but I would not ship a hand-rolled version
twice.
