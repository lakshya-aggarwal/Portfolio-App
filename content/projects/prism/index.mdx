## The problem

Colour-extraction tools give you the five most common colours in an image. They
almost never tell you that four of them fail contrast against each other, which
is the only thing you actually needed to know.

## Approach

Extraction runs as a fragment shader over a downsampled texture - k-means in
Oklab rather than RGB, so perceptual distance matches what the eye reports. Every
candidate pair is contrast-scored before it reaches the UI, and anything that
can't clear AA at body size is never offered as a text colour.

## What I'd do differently

Oklab from the beginning. The RGB version produced palettes that were
numerically diverse and visually muddy, and I lost a week to that.
